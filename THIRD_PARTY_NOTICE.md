## h3-Open-SYS/WaitIO

WaitIO.jl requires an independently supplied installation of h3-Open-SYS/WaitIO
for communication. As of November 2026, upstream WaitIO is not open source and
its source code is not publicly distributed. It is subject to the upstream
provider's separate, restrictive terms, not the MIT license of this Julia package.
Users must obtain access and applicable terms from the provider or computing
facility. This repository does not distribute upstream WaitIO libraries or headers
and does not grant rights to redistribute them.

## MPI

The selected WaitIO installation uses its site's MPI implementation. MPI libraries
are supplied separately and remain subject to their respective licenses and terms.
